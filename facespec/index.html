Chicago with Carthage behind it

the smallest change: the marks Chicago lacks fall to a Mac face, not to Helvetica
Cythera Data › Components › Actors › Magpie
34 angle quotes, 18 middots and 16 en dashes are set in something else today. Carthage carries the first two: › and ·. The en dash – is in neither and still falls through.
The data file's own styles: Sys Large and Lang0 are ArgosANouveau; Labels, Stats and Text are Geneva 9, 9 and 10; the runes are Seldane 10, 12 and 18. The application's fallback set is Chicago 12, Geneva 9 and 10, Espy Sans 10 to 18. Carthage Sans is Brian Connors's reimagining of Espy Sans, dual licensed OFL and CC-BY-SA 4.0.
